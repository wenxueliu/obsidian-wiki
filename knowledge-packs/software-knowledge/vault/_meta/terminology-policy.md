# Terminology Policy

`terms/` owns canonical business terminology. A term page records its canonical name, aliases, definition, domain, boundary, and concepts it must not be confused with.

- Search canonical names and aliases before proposing a term.
- An agent may create a staged candidate term, but must not automatically merge, rename, deprecate, or change the core definition of a confirmed term.
- Unconfirmed equivalence stays ambiguous. Do not merge two terms because a single source says they may be the same.
- When source language conflicts with a confirmed term, preserve both sources and request review rather than rewriting the confirmed definition.
- Technical vocabulary without business ownership belongs in `concepts/`, not `terms/`.
- Use the canonical term in other pages and add an existing Wikilink. If the term page is only proposed, record it in `pending_relations`.
