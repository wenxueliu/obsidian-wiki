# Book Knowledge Rules

- Every page has one primary category and the required traceability frontmatter.
- `books/` represents works, not physical copies; editions and translations remain fields or sources.
- `arguments/` contains attributed theses. Do not present a book's claim as verified fact without independent support.
- Chapter, section, and quotation details are source locators, not standalone pages.
- Reusable conclusions spanning multiple books belong in `synthesis/` and link back to their sources.
- Use `reading/` for dated reading activity, `concepts/` for abstract ideas, and `practices/` for actionable methods.
- Search for an existing identity before creating a page; merge or link rather than duplicate.
